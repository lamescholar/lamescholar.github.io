My guide to research any topic
<br><br>

First, look at the review articles. They usually have bibliographies in the end. You can find review articles in encyclopedias. See [Dictionaries](/en/dictionaries).

Wikipedia articles almost always have bibliography.

DuckDuckGo spits out good materials, here you can also find literature on your topic.

<https://duckduckgo.com/>

Finally, look up your topic into this universal bibliography:

<https://rutracker.org/forum/viewtopic.php?t=6314400>
