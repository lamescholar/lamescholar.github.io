# Dictionaries on historical principles
<br>

First edition of famous Oxford English Dictionary was called<br>
A New English Dictionary on Historical Principles<br>
What principles? 

Dictionary on historical principles means that the dictionary collects the uses of the word from oldest records up to modern use. So you can trace the meaning of the word and find a lot of cool quotes from old books.

Most European languages have a dictionary on historical principles. Here I will list the ones I found. For me they are interesting as a way to find ancient books in foreign languages. Each word is a rabbit hole.
<br><br>

#### English

World English Historical Dictionary - <https://wehd.com/>

That's the first edition of OED.

Oxford English Dictionary - <https://www.oed.com/>

To access this site you need institutional subscription.<br>
There's an MDX dictionary out there.

Historical Dictionary of Science Fiction - <https://sfdictionary.com/>
<br><br>

#### French

Trésor de la Langue Française - <http://atilf.atilf.fr/>

There's a DSL dictionary out there.
<br><br>

#### German

Woerterbuchnetz - <https://woerterbuchnetz.de/?sigle=DWB#0>

This portal hosts Grimm Brothers' Deutsches Wörterbuch and other German dictionaries.
<br><br>

#### Spanish

Diccionario histórico de la lengua española - <https://www.rae.es/dhle/>
